How we score Polymarket traders

Wakeloom's selection rules and stats: a 90-day window, resolved markets, drawdown and concentration, with wallets ranked by composite score.

What we measure

The window is the last 90 days and only resolved markets count. For each wallet we show win rate, ROI, max drawdown, resolved markets, average entry price, median trade size and median holding time.

ROI = realized P&L ÷ capital invested, excluding unrealized P&L on open positions. Some addresses use third-party data, and the page shows the source. For those, ROI is the provider's figure and may be calculated differently.

Wallets with too few trades are too small a sample and get no detail page.

Score first, then rank

Wakeloom scores addresses using checks such as single-event concentration and wash trading; addresses that fail get no score, and likely market makers are not ranked. Some addresses use third-party data, and the page shows the source, so please review them yourself.

Wallets that pass are ranked by long-term results. They are ranked by composite score. Win rate and ROI stay on the board as display columns, not as the sort key. The site and the app read the same public leaderboard, including the as-of time.

Sources and updates

Fills come from Polygon on-chain records and Polymarket's public APIs. Website pages are rebuilt daily and show their data time in your local timezone. Values we can't get show "No data" and are never filled with 0.

Limitations

Past 90-day results don't predict the future. The filters remove obvious noise but not all luck, and they can't see whether the person behind a wallet has changed strategy.

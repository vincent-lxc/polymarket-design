About Wakeloom

Wakeloom is a wallet analytics and paper-trading tool for Polymarket. The name comes from a ship's wake: the traces traders leave on-chain, woven into data you can read.

Phase one covers wallet analytics and paper copy trading; more strategy types will follow.

Privacy

Website pages show only public on-chain wallet data. When you use the app we store your wallet address, sign-in session and the paper-trading rules you set, to provide the service.

When you sign in with Google or email, we collect your email address. Sign-in information is hosted by Privy.

We never collect or store your private key, and we don't sell personal data. Contact us to delete your data.

No one-off whales or wash volume

Wallets whose results hinge on one event, whose volume dwarfs their results, or that self-cross or make markets are excluded.

↗02

A data page for every wallet

90-day win rate, ROI, max drawdown, resolved markets and equity curve. Missing values say "No data".

Paper trading counts the costs

Proportional mirroring with caps and a deviation limit; every trade logs its price gap and delay.

How to use it

  1. 1Pick a wallet from the leaderboard's long-term data
  2. 2Set rules: ratio, per-trade cap, per-market cap, deviation limit
  3. 3Run paper trading: log price gap, delay and net result per trade
  4. 4Then decide: consider small live size only if the numbers hold up

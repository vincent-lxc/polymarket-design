Why paper trading first

Copying always lands a little behind the wallet you follow: spotting their fill takes time, the price moves meanwhile, and fees come out on top. Paper trading for a while shows what is left after those costs before you decide anything else.

Who it's for

People who want to study a Polymarket wallet's track record without putting real money in first.

Rules you set

Ratio: we size each order as a proportion of theirs.

Caps: a per-trade cap and a per-market cap; anything above is cut.

Deviation limit: if our price is too far from their fill, we skip the trade.

Follow the close: when they reduce or exit, we do the same proportionally.

Global stop (coming soon): pause all copying at once.

How it runs

When we see their fill, we simulate ours against the live order book and log the price gap, delay and fees. After one to two weeks we look at the net result after costs before considering live trading. Live copy trading is coming soon.

Limitations

Phase one is paper trading only; no real orders are placed. Simulated fills are estimated from the book and live fills may be worse.
